Connected & Automated Mobility

Lorenzo Italiano

PhD Student · Politecnico di Milano

My research focuses on smart road infrastructures for connected and automated vehicles, spanning radio localization, location privacy preservation, and advanced V2X services. The goal is to turn connectivity and infrastructure-generated data into practical enablers for safer and more automated mobility.
